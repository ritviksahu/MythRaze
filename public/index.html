Why It Works

Built for practical review flows, not just a single-screen prototype

The interface, workflow, and evidence presentation are designed to make technical output feel approachable and trustworthy.

Backend-Assisted Verdicts

The client extracts forensic features and the backend generates the official case verdict, signal breakdown, and report object.

Persistent Case History

Every analysis is stored server-side, making it possible to review older investigations during a live demo or evaluation across multiple connected clients.

Operational Explainability

The system does not stop at a score. It returns pipeline steps, evidence notes, and next-step recommendations for reviewers.

Zero-Dependency Deployment

It runs with `npm start`, needs no external services, and is easy to host or submit as a complete hackathon project. Use `HOST=0.0.0.0` when you want other devices on the network to connect.
